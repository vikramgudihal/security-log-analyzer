Report Specification Checklist

11/11 Covered

The generated report complies with all 11 required sections from the task guidelines:

  1. Executive Summary: Overall risk posture and high-level assessment.
  2. Analysis Metadata: Log filename, execution time, and UTC timezone.
  3. Total Events: Breakdown of total lines, successful and failed logins.
  4. Successful Logins: Authorized authentication sessions.
  5. Failed Logins: Authentication errors and burst counts.
  6. Suspicious IP Addresses: Detailed table of flagged hosts and severity.
  7. Detected Anomalies: Chronological record of rule violations.
  8. Severity Classification: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, and CRITICAL breakdown.
  9. Relevant Event Evidence: Sample raw log lines demonstrating detections.
  10. Recommended Investigation Steps: Containment and hardening guidelines.
  11. False-Positive Disclaimer: Explaining behavioral indicator limits.
